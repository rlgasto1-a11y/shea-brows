ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Shea

Brow artist. Detail lover. Confidence builder.

Based in Birmingham, Alabama, I’ve always had a love for beauty and helping people feel good about themselves. For me, brows aren’t just about the perfect shape — they’re about bringing out what’s already naturally beautiful about you.

I take the time to understand what each client wants and create brows that fit their face, style, and personality. Whether you like them soft and natural or more defined, my goal is for you to leave feeling confident and loving your brows.

Birmingham is home, and I’m proud to create a space where every client feels comfortable, welcomed, and beautiful.
